## Portugal

- [Casa da Arquitectura — Centro Português de Arquitectura (Portuguese Centre for Architecture)](https://www.casadaarquitectura.pt/en/) - Architecture archive and museum in Matosinhos holding the archives of Paulo Mendes da Rocha and Lucio Costa; bilingual digital archive. *Free online, paid entry.*
- [Fundação Marques da Silva (Marques da Silva Foundation)](https://fims.up.pt/) - University of Porto foundation with the archives of José Marques da Silva, Fernando Távora and other architects. *Free.*
- [Ordem dos Arquitectos (Portuguese Order of Architects)](https://www.ordemdosarquitectos.org/) - Professional body; publisher of Arquitectura Popular em Portugal. *Free.*
- [ArchDaily — Portugal](https://www.archdaily.com/country/portugal) - Contemporary Portuguese architecture projects. *Free.*
