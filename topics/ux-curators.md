- [Jared Spool](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jmspool/) - UX strategy and training UX teams; see [Center Centre](https://www.centercentre.com/). *Free.*
- [Indi Young](https://www.linkedin.com/in/indiyoung/) - Mental models and listening sessions; see [indiyoung.com](https://indiyoung.com/). *Free.*
- [Teresa Torres](https://www.linkedin.com/in/teresatorres/) - Continuous discovery and interviewing; see [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/). *Free.*
- [Erika Hall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/erikahall/) - Just enough research; see [Mule Design](https://www.muledesign.com/). *Free.*
- [Ioana Teleanu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ioanateleanu/) - AI and UX, design careers. *Free.*
- [Andy Budd](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andybudd/) - Design leadership and design maturity. *Free.*
