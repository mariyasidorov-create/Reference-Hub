## Archives and Image Collections

- [BNDigital](https://bndigital.bnportugal.gov.pt) - Digital National Library of Portugal: books, maps, prints. *Mixed licences.*
- [Digitarq](https://digitarq.arquivos.pt) - Portuguese state archives, including Torre do Tombo. *Mixed licences.*
- [Arquivo Municipal de Lisboa](https://arquivomunicipal.cm-lisboa.pt) - Lisbon photo archive from the 19th century onwards. *Mixed licences.*
- [Hemeroteca Digital de Lisboa](https://hemerotecadigital.cm-lisboa.pt) - Digitised Portuguese newspapers and magazines, including Ilustração Portuguesa. *Mixed licences.*
- [MatrizNet](http://www.matriznet.dgpc.pt) - Collections of Portuguese state museums, including azulejos. *View only.*
- [Restos de Colecção](https://restosdecoleccao.blogspot.com) - History of Portuguese shops, brands and signage in photos. *View only.*

## Design Museum and Libraries

- [MUDE — Museu do Design (Design Museum, Lisbon)](https://mude.pt/) - Lisbon's design museum on Rua Augusta: graphic and product design, fashion, jewellery; visitable storage of graphic design. *Free on Fridays after 17:00 and Sunday mornings.*
- [MUDE — Biblioteca e Centro de Documentação (MUDE Library and Documentation Centre)](https://nyronmude.cm-lisboa.pt/catalog/) - Design library catalogue with 12 special collections (Centro Português de Design, João Machado, Helena Cardoso, Miguel Rios Design and others) and a historical archive. *Free, reading room by appointment.*
- [MUDE — Acervo Online (MUDE Online Collection)](https://inwebmude.cm-lisboa.pt/) - Searchable museum collection; prefixes MUDE.G (graphic design), MUDE.P (product), MUDE.M (fashion), MUDE.J (jewellery). *Free.*
- [Biblioteca de Arte Gulbenkian (Gulbenkian Art Library)](https://gulbenkian.pt/biblioteca-arte/) - Portugal's main art library with about 180 photographic collections, many digitised. *Free.*
- [Biblioteca de Arte Gulbenkian on Flickr (Gulbenkian Art Library on Flickr)](https://www.flickr.com/people/26577438@N06) - Photos from Estúdio Mário Novais, Portuguese azulejos and gilt woodwork, Amadeo de Souza-Cardoso's estate. *CC BY 4.0 for most images.*
- [Imprensa Nacional — Coleção D (Portuguese Designers Series)](https://imprensanacional.pt/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/D8-LMC-WEB.pdf) - Monographs on Portuguese designers such as Victor Palla, Sebastião Rodrigues, Paulo-Guilherme and Roberto Nobre; publisher PDFs. *Free PDF from the publisher.*

## Magazines and Modernism

- [Hemeroteca Digital de Lisboa (Lisbon Digital Newspaper Library)](https://hemerotecadigital.cm-lisboa.pt/) - Digitised newspapers and magazines, including Ilustração Portuguesa (1903–1993) and Contemporânea. *Free PDF.*
- [Revistas de Ideias e Cultura (Magazines of Ideas and Culture)](https://pt.revistasdeideias.net/) - Fully digitised 20th-century magazines: Orpheu, Contemporânea, A Águia, Seara Nova, O Tempo e o Modo. *Free.*
- [Modernismo — Arquivo Virtual da Geração de Orpheu (Virtual Archive of the Orpheu Generation)](https://modernismo.pt/) - Archives of Almada Negreiros, Sarah Affonso and the Orpheu writers, with magazine covers and graphic work. *Free.*
- [Casa Comum — Diário de Lisboa (Lisbon Daily Archive)](https://casacomum.org/) - Every issue of Diário de Lisboa, 1921–1990, plus other archives. *Free.*
- [Purl.pt — Biblioteca Nacional Digital (National Digital Library)](https://purl.pt/) - Digitised books, prints, maps and posters from the National Library of Portugal. *Free.*

## Posters and Ephemera

- [Ephemera — Biblioteca e Arquivo de José Pacheco Pereira (Ephemera Library and Archive)](https://ephemerajpp.com/) - Largest private archive in Portugal: tens of thousands of posters, pamphlets, stickers and photos. *Free to browse.*

## Azulejo

- [Az Infinitum — Sistema de Referência e Indexação de Azulejo (Azulejo Indexation and Referencing System)](https://redeazulejo.letras.ulisboa.pt/pesquisa-az/) - Largest database of tiles produced or applied in Portugal, with the National Azulejo Museum. *Free.*

## Type Foundries

- [R-Typography](https://www.r-typography.com/) - Portuguese type foundry. *Paid.*
- [Feliciano Type Foundry](https://www.felicianotypefoundry.com/) - Portuguese type foundry with revivals of Portuguese lettering. *Paid.*

## Museums and Events

- [MAAT — Museu de Arte, Arquitetura e Tecnologia (Museum of Art, Architecture and Technology)](https://www.maat.pt/) - Lisbon museum of contemporary art, architecture and technology. *Paid entry.*
- [Museu Calouste Gulbenkian (Calouste Gulbenkian Museum)](https://gulbenkian.pt/museu/) - Founder's collection and modern Portuguese art (CAM). *Paid entry.*
- [Serralves](https://www.serralves.pt/) - Porto museum of contemporary art in a Siza building with a park. *Paid entry.*
- [Porto Design Biennale](https://portodesignbiennale.pt/) - Biennial design festival in Porto and Matosinhos with an online archive of past editions. *Free.*
- [ESAD — Escola Superior de Artes e Design (School of Art and Design, Matosinhos)](https://esad.pt/) - Design school and research centre (ESAD—idea) behind many exhibitions on Portuguese graphic design. *Free.*

## Lisbon Design Week

- [Lisbon Design Week](https://lisbondesignweek.com/) - Citywide design festival held each May across about 80 venues in 11 neighbourhoods, focused on Portuguese makers and craft. *Free.*
- [Lisbon by Design](https://www.lisbonbydesign.com/) - Collectible design fair that runs alongside Lisbon Design Week. *Paid entry.*
- [Burel Factory](https://burelfactory.com/) - Wool textile mill from Serra da Estrela; a regular Lisbon Design Week participant. *Shop.*
- [Cutipol](https://www.cutipol.pt/en) - Portuguese cutlery brand; a Lisbon Design Week participant. *Shop.*
- [Constança Entrudo](https://www.constancaentrudo.com/) - Textile designer working with modular surfaces; shown at Lisbon Design Week 2026. *Shop.*
