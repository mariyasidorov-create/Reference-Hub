## General Archives and Libraries

- [Monoskop: Avant-garde and modernist magazines](https://monoskop.org/Journals) - Guide to avant-garde magazines of 1900–1940 with links to digitised copies. *Free.*
- [Digital Dada Library (University of Iowa)](https://dada.lib.uiowa.edu/) - Full scans of Dada magazines and related publications. *Free.*
- [Blue Mountain Project (Princeton)](https://bluemountain.princeton.edu/) - Full-text issues of European avant-garde magazines, 1848–1923. *Free.*
- [Letterform Archive Online](https://oa.letterformarchive.org/) - Online collection of lettering, posters, magazines and type specimens in high resolution. *Free.*
- [Design Reviewed](https://designreviewed.com/) - Scans of 20th-century graphic design magazines such as Gebrauchsgraphik, TM and Neue Grafik. *Free, some for members.*
- [People's Graphic Design Archive](https://peoplesgdarchive.org/) - Crowd-sourced archive of graphic design history. *Free.*
- [Museum für Gestaltung Zürich, eMuseum](https://www.emuseum.ch/) - Posters and graphic design, especially the Swiss school. *Free.*
- [Cooper Hewitt Collection](https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/) - Smithsonian design museum collection: graphics, posters, type. *Free.*
- [AIGA Design Archives](https://designarchives.aiga.org/) - Work from AIGA competitions since the 1920s. *Free.*
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) - Books, magazines, films and type specimens; public domain scans and controlled lending. *Free, account for lending.*
- [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/) - Legal digital lending of books, like a library. *Free, account.*
- [Getty Publications Virtual Library](https://www.getty.edu/publications/virtuallibrary/) - Hundreds of Getty books as free, legal PDFs. *Free.*
- [Eye Magazine archive](https://www.eyemagazine.com/) - Articles from Eye magazine on graphic design history and criticism. *Partly free.*
- [Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/) - Archive of typefaces in real-world work, including many historical pieces. *Free.*
- [Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/) - Digitised German libraries, archives and museums, including Gebrauchsgraphik and type specimens. *Free.*
- [Heidelberg historic literature – digitized](https://digi.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/) - Digitised Ver Sacrum, Jugend, Deutsche Kunst und Dekoration and manuscripts. *Free.*
- [НЭБ — Национальная электронная библиотека (National Electronic Library of Russia)](https://rusneb.ru/) - Russian publications: Niva magazine, type specimens, avant-garde books. *Free, some in reading rooms.*

## Medieval Manuscripts and Calligraphy

- [e-codices](https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/) - Swiss manuscripts, including the Abbey Library of St. Gallen. *Free.*
- [Gallica (BnF)](https://gallica.bnf.fr/) - French manuscripts, incunabula and L'ornementation des manuscrits. *Free.*
- [Digital Bodleian](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/) - Oxford manuscripts and printed ephemera. *Free.*
- [Mira Calligraphiae Monumenta (Getty)](https://www.getty.edu/publications/) - Calligraphy by Georg Bocskay and Joris Hoefnagel for the court of Rudolf II. *Paid.*
- [Christopher de Hamel — Meetings with Remarkable Manuscripts](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/) - Twelve great manuscripts from the Gospels to the late Middle Ages. *Paid.*

## Type, Specimens and Printing

- [D. B. Updike — Printing Types (1922)](https://archive.org/search?query=Updike+Printing+Types) - Classic history of printing types, in the public domain. *Free.*
- [Edward Johnston — Manuscript and Inscription Letters (1909)](https://archive.org/search?query=Johnston+Manuscript+and+Inscription+Letters) - Johnston's lettering plates, in the public domain. *Free.*
- [F. W. Goudy — The Alphabet and Elements of Lettering (1918)](https://archive.org/search?query=Goudy+Alphabet+Elements+of+Lettering) - Goudy's lettering textbook, in the public domain. *Free.*
- [Type Specimens on Internet Archive](https://archive.org/search?query=type+specimen) - Specimens from German, American and French foundries such as Klingspor, Stempel and ATF. *Free.*
- [Klingspor Museum (Offenbach)](https://www.klingspormuseum.de/) - Museum of type and the book; archive of the Klingspor foundry. *Free.*
- [Hamilton Wood Type & Printing Museum](https://woodtype.org/) - Wood type and 19th-century American printing. *Free.*
- [Cees W. de Jong — Type: A Visual History of Typefaces & Graphic Styles](https://www.taschen.com/) - Two volumes of type specimens from 1628 to 1938. *Paid.*
- [U&lc Back Issues](https://fontsinuse.com/tags/1309/uandlc) - ITC's magazine art-directed by Herb Lubalin, 1973–1999; Monotype released the PDFs for free. *Free.*

## Victorian Era and Arts and Crafts

- [Library of Congress Prints & Photographs](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/) - 19th-century circus and theatre posters, WPA posters. *Free.*
