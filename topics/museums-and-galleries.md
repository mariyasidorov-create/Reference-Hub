## Museums in the UK

- [Victoria and Albert Museum (V&A)](https://www.vam.ac.uk/) - The world's leading museum of art, design and performance, with V&A East and V&A Dundee. *Free.*
- [Design Museum](https://designmuseum.org/) - London museum of contemporary design and architecture. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/) - Two million years of human history, from Egypt to Japan. *Free.*
- [National Gallery](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/) - Western European painting from the 13th to the 20th century. *Free.*
- [National Portrait Gallery](https://www.npg.org.uk/) - Portraits of people who shaped British history and culture. *Free.*
- [Tate](https://www.tate.org.uk/) - Tate Britain, Tate Modern, Tate Liverpool and Tate St Ives. *Free.*
- [Royal Academy of Arts](https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/) - Artist-led academy with exhibitions and its own collection and library. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Somerset House](https://www.somersethouse.org.uk/) - Arts centre on the Thames for design, fashion and contemporary culture. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [The Wallace Collection](https://www.wallacecollection.org/) - Old Masters, French 18th-century art and arms in a London townhouse. *Free.*
- [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/) - Museum and library about health and the human experience. *Free.*
- [Museum of the Home](https://www.museumofthehome.org.uk/) - How people lived at home from 1600 to today: interiors room by room. *Free.*
- [Horniman Museum and Gardens](https://www.horniman.ac.uk/) - Anthropology, musical instruments and natural history in south London. *Free.*
- [Fitzwilliam Museum](https://fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/) - Cambridge University museum of art and antiquities. *Free.*
- [Ashmolean Museum](https://www.ashmolean.org/) - Oxford University museum of art and archaeology, founded 1683. *Free.*
- [Fashion Museum Bath](https://www.fashionmuseum.co.uk/) - Fashion and dress from the 17th century onwards. *Paid entry.*
- [Watts Gallery](https://www.wattsgallery.org.uk/) - Artists' village of G. F. and Mary Watts, Arts and Crafts chapel. *Paid entry.*
- [Dorich House Museum](https://dorichhousemuseum.org.uk/) - Sculptor Dora Gordine's modernist studio house. *Paid entry.*
- [Historic Royal Palaces](https://www.hrp.org.uk/) - Tower of London, Hampton Court, Kensington Palace and others. *Paid entry.*

## Museums in Europe

- [Musée du Louvre](https://www.louvre.fr/en) - Paris museum from antiquity to the 19th century. *Paid entry.*
- [Musée de l'Orangerie](https://www.musee-orangerie.fr/en) - Monet's Water Lilies and the Walter-Guillaume collection, Paris. *Paid entry.*
- [Rijksmuseum](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en) - Dutch art and history, Amsterdam; huge free image collection. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Van Gogh Museum](https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/en) - The largest collection of Van Gogh, Amsterdam. *Paid entry.*
- [Museo Guggenheim Bilbao](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/en) - Frank Gehry's building and modern and contemporary art. *Paid entry.*
- [Garage Museum of Contemporary Art (Музей современного искусства «Гараж»)](https://garagemca.org/en) - Moscow museum in a Soviet pavilion rebuilt by OMA, with a library, archive and publishing programme. *Free.*

## Museums in the United States

- [The Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met)](https://www.metmuseum.org/) - New York encyclopedic museum of 5,000 years of art. *Paid entry.*
- [Whitney Museum of American Art](https://whitney.org/) - 20th and 21st-century American art, New York. *Paid entry.*
- [The Frick Collection](https://www.frick.org/) - Old Masters and decorative arts in Henry Clay Frick's mansion. *Paid entry.*
- [Neue Galerie New York](https://www.neuegalerie.org/) - Early 20th-century German and Austrian art and design: Klimt, Wiener Werkstätte. *Paid entry.*
- [Brooklyn Museum](https://www.brooklynmuseum.org/) - Egyptian, African, American and feminist art. *Paid entry.*
- [Getty](https://www.getty.edu/) - Getty Center and Villa, Los Angeles, plus research institute. *Free.*

## Online Collections and Publications

- [British Museum Collection Online](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection) - Millions of objects with images, many free for non-commercial use. *Free.*
- [National Gallery: Search the Collection](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/search-the-collection) - Every painting with high-resolution images. *Free.*
- [Tate: Art and Artists](https://www.tate.org.uk/art) - Search Tate's collection of British and international art. *Free.*
- [Royal Academy Collection](https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/art-artists/search/search-the-collection) - Works, archives and library of the Royal Academy. *Free.*
- [National Portrait Gallery Collections](https://www.npg.org.uk/collections) - Over 250,000 portraits searchable online. *Free.*
- [Wellcome Collection Catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/collections) - Digitised books, manuscripts and images, many in the public domain. *Free.*
- [Louvre Collections](https://collections.louvre.fr/en/) - Database of almost 500,000 Louvre works. *Free.*
- [Van Gogh: The Letters](https://vangoghletters.org/) - All of Van Gogh's letters with sketches, translated and annotated. *Free.*
- [The Met Collection](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection) - Search the Met's collection; Open Access images are public domain. *Free.*
- [MetPublications](https://www.metmuseum.org/met-publications) - Hundreds of Met exhibition catalogues to read or download. *Free.*
- [MoMA Collection](https://www.moma.org/collection/) - Painting, design, photography and film in MoMA's collection. *Free.*
- [Guggenheim Collection Online](https://www.guggenheim.org/collection-online) - Works and artist essays from the Guggenheim collection. *Free.*
- [Guggenheim Publications](https://www.guggenheim.org/publications) - Exhibition catalogues, some free to read online. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Whitney Collection](https://whitney.org/collection/works) - Search the Whitney collection of American art. *Free.*
- [Getty Museum Collection](https://www.getty.edu/art/collection/) - Getty collection; Open Content images free to use. *Free.*
- [Historic England Archive](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/) - Photographs and plans of English buildings and places. *Free.*
- [Arts Council Collection](https://artscouncilcollection.org.uk/) - Britain's national loan collection of modern and contemporary art. *Free.*
- [Art UK](https://artuk.org/) - Every oil painting and sculpture in UK public collections, searchable by artist and place. *Free.*

## Galleries and Art Centres

- [Saatchi Gallery](https://www.saatchigallery.com/) - Contemporary art gallery in Chelsea, London. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Serpentine](https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/) - Contemporary art in Kensington Gardens and the yearly Serpentine Pavilion. *Free.*
- [Hayward Gallery](https://www.southbankcentre.co.uk/venues/hayward-gallery) - Brutalist gallery at London's Southbank Centre. *Paid entry.*
- [Whitechapel Gallery](https://www.whitechapelgallery.org/) - East London public gallery since 1901. *Free.*
- [Ikon Gallery](https://www.ikon-gallery.org/) - Contemporary art in Birmingham. *Free.*
- [Studio Voltaire](https://www.studiovoltaire.org/) - Non-profit gallery and studios in south London. *Free.*
- [Art on the Underground](https://art.tfl.gov.uk/) - Contemporary art commissions across the London Underground. *Free.*

## Art Magazines and Fairs

- [The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/) - News and analysis about the art world. *Partly paywalled.*
- [ArtReview](https://artreview.com/) - International contemporary art magazine. *Partly paywalled.*
- [Frieze](https://www.frieze.com/) - Contemporary art magazine and fairs in London, New York, Seoul and Los Angeles. *Partly paywalled.*
- [Artforum](https://www.artforum.com/) - Contemporary art criticism since 1962. *Partly paywalled.*
- [ARTnews](https://www.artnews.com/) - The oldest US art magazine. *Partly paywalled.*
- [Art in America](https://www.artnews.com/c/art-in-america/) - American art criticism, now published with ARTnews. *Partly paywalled.*
- [Artnet News](https://news.artnet.com/) - Art market and art world news. *Free.*
- [The Burlington Magazine](https://www.burlington.org.uk/) - Scholarly monthly on fine and decorative arts since 1903. *Subscription.*
- [Artsy](https://www.artsy.net/) - Database of artists, galleries and artworks with editorial. *Free.*
- [Art Basel](https://www.artbasel.com/) - Art fairs in Basel, Miami Beach, Hong Kong and Paris. *Paid entry.*
- [ICON](https://www.iconeye.com/) - London magazine on architecture and design. *Partly paywalled.*
- [Stack](https://www.stackmagazines.com/) - Subscription service and guide to independent magazines. *Shop.*
- [Persephone Books](https://persephonebooks.co.uk/) - Reprints of neglected 20th-century writers with designed endpapers. *Shop.*

## Learning about Art

- [Sotheby's Institute of Art](https://www.sothebysinstitute.com/) - Degrees and short courses in art business and art history. *Paid entry.*
- [The Arts Society](https://theartssociety.org/) - Lectures on art and decorative arts across the UK. *Paid entry.*

## Museums in Scotland

- [V&A Dundee](https://www.vam.ac.uk/dundee) - Scotland's design museum in a building by Kengo Kuma, with the Scottish Design Galleries. *Free.*
- [National Galleries of Scotland](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/) - Scottish National Gallery, Portrait Gallery and Modern Art galleries in Edinburgh. *Free.*
- [National Galleries of Scotland: Art and Artists](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/art-and-artists) - Search the national collection, including the Glasgow Boys and Colourists. *Free.*
- [Glasgow Museums (Kelvingrove, Burrell Collection)](https://www.glasgowlife.org.uk/museums) - City collections with the largest public holding of the Glasgow Boys and Mackintosh. *Free.*
- [The Hunterian](https://www.gla.ac.uk/hunterian/) - University of Glasgow museum with the Mackintosh House and Whistler collection. *Free.*
- [National Museums Scotland](https://www.nms.ac.uk/) - National Museum of Scotland in Edinburgh: design, fashion, decorative arts. *Free.*
- [The Glasgow School of Art Archives and Collections](https://www.gsaarchives.net/) - Mackintosh drawings, student work and school records. *Free.*
- [Charles Rennie Mackintosh Society](https://www.crmsociety.com/) - Mackintosh Queen's Cross church and a guide to Mackintosh buildings. *Free.*
