## Tools

- [Runway](https://runwayml.com/) - AI video generation and editing. *Freemium.*
- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/) - Image and video generation. *Paid.*
- [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/) - AI avatar video. *Paid.*
- [Vizcom](https://www.vizcom.ai/) - Sketch to render for industrial design. *Freemium.*
- [Teachable Machine (Google)](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/) - Train simple ML models in the browser. *Free.*
- [Magenta (Google)](https://magenta.tensorflow.org/) - Machine learning for music and art. *Free.*
- [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) - JavaScript 3D library for the web. *Free.*
- [Shader Gradient](https://shadergradient.co/) - Animated 3D gradients for web and Figma. *Free.*
- [Figma Weave](https://weave.figma.com/) - Node-based AI workflows for image, video and 3D (formerly Weavy). *Freemium.*
- [Krea](https://www.krea.ai/) - Real-time AI image, video and 3D generation. *Freemium.*
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) - AI voice, sound effects and music. *Freemium.*
- [Suno](https://suno.com/) - AI music generation. *Freemium.*
- [LTX Studio](https://ltx.studio/) - AI storyboards and video production. *Freemium.*
- [Kling AI](https://klingai.com/) - AI video generation. *Freemium.*
- [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai/) - AI video and image studio with camera presets. *Freemium.*
- [Muzli MCP](https://muz.li/blog/muzli-mcp-design-with-ai/) - Connects Claude to Muzli's curated collections for moodboards, palettes and section design from references. *Free with account.*
- [Genery](https://genery.io/) - Moodboards and cinematic references for creators. *Freemium.*

## Artists and Experiments

- [Refik Anadol](https://refikanadol.com/) - Data-driven AI art installations. *Free.*
- [Sougwen Chung (愫君)](https://sougwen.com/) - Artist drawing in collaboration with robots. *Free.*
- [Experiments with Google](https://experiments.withgoogle.com/) - Creative coding and AI experiments. *Free.*
- [Gather (DAO)](https://gather.be-of-dao.com/) - Browser toy that turns up to four photos into interactive depth-mapped particle clouds: drift, swarm, scan, trails. *Free.*
- [Inflation-2 (Nikita Vasilevskiy)](https://nikitavasilevskiy.com/inflation-2) - Draw a shape and inflate it into a glossy 3D object; symmetry, holes, reflections, glow; export PNG, video or USDZ. *Free.*
- [Artbreeder](https://www.artbreeder.com/) - Collaborative image breeding and remixing. *Freemium.*
- [Deep Dream Generator](https://deepdreamgenerator.com/) - Early neural art tool, still running. *Freemium.*
- [NVIDIA AI Playground](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/research/ai-playground/) - NVIDIA Research demos of generative AI. *Free.*
- [Magic Sketchpad](https://magic-sketchpad.glitch.me/) - Google Magenta demo that completes your doodles. *Free.*

## AI for Visuals: Image, Video and 3D

- [Tripo AI](https://www.tripo3d.ai/) - Image or text to 3D models, export to GLB and other formats. Seen at Kris Anfalova and Jan Mraz. *Free + paid.*
- [Hitem3D](https://hitem3d.ai/) - Image to high-detail 3D models. *Free + paid.*
- [Lovart](https://www.lovart.ai/) - AI design agent for brand and campaign visuals. *Free + paid.*
- [Kittl](https://www.kittl.com/) - Graphic design editor with AI for posters, merch and type effects. *Free + paid.*
- [Luma AI (Dream Machine)](https://lumalabs.ai/) - Text and image to video. *Free + paid.*
- [Seedance (ByteDance Seed)](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedance) - Video generation model used through Higgsfield and other apps. *Via partner apps.*
- [InVideo](https://invideo.io/) - AI video editor and generator. Used by Tseily. *Free + paid.*
- [Artlist](https://artlist.io/) - Licensed music, SFX, footage and AI video tools. *Subscription.*
- [VEED](https://www.veed.io/) - Browser video editor with AI captions and avatars. *Free + paid.*
- [Arcads](https://www.arcads.ai/) - AI UGC video ads with actors. *Paid.*
- [Topview](https://www.topview.ai/) - AI video agent for product and marketing videos. *Free + paid.*
- [Freepik AI Suite](https://www.freepik.com/) - Stock plus AI image and video generation. *Free + paid.*
- [Pacdora](https://www.pacdora.com/) - Packaging mockups and dielines in 3D. *Free + paid.*
- [Genspark](https://www.genspark.ai/) - AI agent for slides, docs, images and video. *Free + paid.*
- [Google Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/) - Google's AI UI design tool, exports to Figma and code. *Free.*
- [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/) - Google's research notebook: chat with sources, audio and video overviews. *Free + paid.*
- [Kimi](https://www.kimi.com/) - Moonshot AI's open-weight models and agent. *Free + paid.*
