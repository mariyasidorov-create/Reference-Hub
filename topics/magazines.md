## Design Magazines

- [Monocle](https://monocle.com/) - Global affairs, design and urbanism magazine, radio and books. *Paid.*
- [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/) - UK magazine on design, advertising and visual culture. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/) - Daily writing on graphic design, illustration and art. *Free.*
- [AIGA Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/) - AIGA's magazine on emerging graphic designers. *Free.*
- [Communication Arts](https://www.commarts.com/) - US magazine and annuals of visual communication. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [PRINT](https://www.printmag.com/) - American graphic design magazine since 1940. *Free.*
- [Brand New (UnderConsideration)](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/) - Daily reviews of new logos and identities. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Slanted](https://www.slanted.de/) - German typography and graphic design magazine and blog. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [IDEA (アイデア)](http://www.idea-mag.com/en/) - Japanese graphic design and typography magazine since 1953. *Paid.*

## Architecture and Interiors

- [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/) - Architecture and design news. *Free.*
- [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/) - Design, interiors, fashion and travel. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Domus](https://www.domusweb.it/en.html) - Italian architecture and design magazine founded by Gio Ponti in 1928. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Apartamento](https://www.apartamentomagazine.com/) - Everyday-life interiors magazine. *Paid.*
- [Kinfolk](https://www.kinfolk.com/) - Slow-living magazine with a recognisable editorial style. *Paid.*

## Shops and Newspapers

- [magCulture](https://magculture.com/) - London shop and journal about independent magazines. *Paid.*
- [The Guardian: Art and Design](https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign) - Free art, design and architecture journalism. *Free.*
