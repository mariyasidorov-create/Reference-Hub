## Stock Photos

- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) - Largest free stock photo library. *Free, Unsplash+ paid.*
- [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) - Free photos and videos. *Free.*
- [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com) - Photos, illustrations and videos; AI images can be filtered out. *Free.*
- [Burst](https://burst.shopify.com) - 20,000 free photos from Shopify, many for e-commerce. *Free.*
- [Kaboompics](https://kaboompics.com) - Lifestyle and interior photos, each with a colour palette. *Free.*
- [StockSnap](https://stocksnap.io) - CC0 photos. *CC0.*
- [Gratisography](https://gratisography.com) - Quirky and funny photos. *Free.*
- [Life of Pix](https://www.lifeofpix.com) - Atmospheric photos from an agency. *Free.*
- [Nappy](https://nappy.co) - Photos of Black people and diverse teams. *Free.*
- [Dupe](https://dupephotos.com) - Independent stock library without AI or ads. *Free.*
- [Reshot](https://www.reshot.com) - Unstaged photos and icons. *Free.*
- [New Old Stock](https://nos.twnsnd.co) - Vintage photos from public archives. *Public domain.*

## Search Across Archives

- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) - More than 100 million freely licensed files. *CC / Public domain.*
- [WikiArt](https://www.wikiart.org) - Encyclopedia of painting by artist and style. *Mixed licences.*
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org) - Books, magazines, films and type specimens; public domain scans and controlled lending. *Mixed licences.*
- [Internet Archive Book Images](https://www.flickr.com/photos/internetarchivebookimages) - Millions of illustrations clipped from old books. *Mostly public domain.*
- [Flickr Commons](https://www.flickr.com/commons) - Photo archives of hundreds of museums and libraries. *No known restrictions.*
- [Openverse](https://openverse.org) - Search across more than 800 million openly licensed images. *CC / Public domain.*
- [Arkivism](https://arkivism.com) - More than 4 million images and videos from 51 archives, searchable by frame. *Public domain, account.*
- [Public Domain Image Archive](https://pdimagearchive.org) - Hand-picked images from The Public Domain Review, updated weekly. *Public domain.*
- [Rawpixel Public Domain](https://www.rawpixel.com/public-domain) - Restored vintage illustrations and photos. *Public domain, some need sign-up.*
- [Europeana](https://www.europeana.eu) - Collections of thousands of European museums and archives. *Filter by licence.*
- [Digital Public Library of America](https://dp.la) - US archives and libraries in one search. *Mixed licences.*

## Open Access Museums

- [Rijksmuseum](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection) - Entire collection in high resolution. *Public domain.*
- [The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search) - 470,000 works with an Open Access filter. *CC0.*
- [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/collection) - Collection with a public domain filter. *CC0.*
- [Smithsonian Open Access](https://www.si.edu/openaccess) - Millions of objects from 19 museums. *CC0.*
- [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/open-access-images.html) - Paintings and works on paper. *CC0.*
- [Cleveland Museum of Art](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access) - Open access collection. *CC0.*
- [Paris Musées](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr) - 14 Paris museums, including photographs and posters. *CC0.*
- [SMK Open](https://www.smk.dk/en/article/smk-open) - National Gallery of Denmark. *Public domain.*
- [LACMA](https://collections.lacma.org) - Los Angeles County Museum of Art collection. *Partly public domain.*
- [Getty Open Content](https://www.getty.edu/projects/open-content-program) - Images from the Getty collections. *Free.*
- [V&A](https://collections.vam.ac.uk) - Design, fashion and decorative arts. *Often non-commercial.*

## Libraries and National Archives

- [Library of Congress — Free to Use](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use) - Themed sets of freely usable images. *Public domain.*
- [NYPL Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org) - Photos, maps, menus, postcards and Victorian ephemera, with a public domain filter. *Public domain filter.*
- [British Library on Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/britishlibrary) - One million illustrations from 19th-century books. *Public domain.*
- [Gallica](https://gallica.bnf.fr) - National Library of France: newspapers, posters, photos, manuscripts. *Free for non-commercial use.*
- [Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de) - Digitised German libraries, archives and museums, including Gebrauchsgraphik and type specimens. *Mixed licences.*
- [MDZ](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de) - Digitised books from the Bavarian State Library. *Mixed licences.*
- [Nationaal Archief](https://www.nationaalarchief.nl) - Dutch national archive with many 20th-century photos. *Partly CC0.*
- [US National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/research/still-pictures) - Photos and graphics of the US government. *Mostly public domain.*
- [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/images) - History of medicine and the human body in images. *Mostly CC BY / Public domain.*
- [Smithsonian Libraries Image Gallery](https://library.si.edu/image-gallery) - Illustrations from rare books. *Mixed licences.*
- [Calisphere](https://calisphere.org) - California archives. *Mixed licences.*
- [Digital Commonwealth](https://www.digitalcommonwealth.org) - Massachusetts archives, with many vintage postcards. *Mixed licences.*

## Specialist Archives

- [Old Book Illustrations](https://www.oldbookillustrations.com) - Engravings and illustrations from old books. *Public domain.*
- [Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org) - Botanical and zoological illustrations. *Mostly public domain.*
- [Plant Illustrations](https://www.plantillustrations.org) - Botanical illustrations only. *Mixed licences.*
- [ukiyo-e.org](https://ukiyo-e.org) - Search across Japanese woodblock prints in museums worldwide. *View only.*
- [David Rumsey Map Collection](https://www.davidrumsey.com) - 150,000 historical maps. *CC BY-NC.*
- [Old Maps Online](https://www.oldmapsonline.org) - Search historical maps by place. *Mixed licences.*
- [Letterform Archive](https://oa.letterformarchive.org) - Archive of lettering, typography and graphic design. *View only.*
- [eMuseum (Museum für Gestaltung Zürich)](https://www.emuseum.ch) - One of the largest poster and design collections, strong on Swiss design. *View only.*
- [Poster House](https://www.posterhouse.org) - Poster museum in New York. *View only.*
- [International Poster Gallery](https://www.internationalposter.com) - Archive of vintage posters by country and theme. *View only (shop).*
- [Numista](https://en.numista.com) - Catalogue of coins and banknotes of the world. *View only.*
- [American Numismatic Society](https://numismatics.org/search) - Museum collection of coins and medals. *Mixed licences.*
- [CoinArchives](https://www.coinarchives.com) - Archive of auction lots with coin photos. *View only.*
- [NASA Image Library](https://images.nasa.gov) - Space, missions and astronauts. *Mostly public domain.*
- [ESA Images](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images) - Photos from the European Space Agency. *Mostly CC BY-SA.*
- [OldNYC](https://www.oldnyc.org) - Historical photos of New York on a map. *From NYPL.*
- [Historypin](https://www.historypin.org) - Historical photos pinned to places. *Mixed licences.*
- [Vintage Everyday](https://www.vintag.es) - Vintage photo collections by theme. *View only.*
