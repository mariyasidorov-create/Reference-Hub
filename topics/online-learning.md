## Courses

- [Graphic Design Specialization (CalArts on Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/graphic-design) - CalArts course on image making, typography and brand. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [UI / UX Design Specialization (CalArts on Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/ui-ux-design) - CalArts course on visual elements, web design and UX research. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [MoMA on Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/moma) - Free MoMA courses on modern art, photography and fashion. *Free.*
- [Google UX Design Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-ux-design) - Entry-level UX course from Google. *Paid.*
- [Interaction Design Foundation](https://www.interaction-design.org/) - UX courses and a free encyclopedia of interaction design. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Domestika](https://www.domestika.org/) - Craft courses from illustration to type design. *Paid.*
- [Open Culture: Free Online Courses](https://www.openculture.com/freeonlinecourses) - Index of free university courses, including art history. *Free.*
- [02UI](https://www.02ui.com/) - Course on designing and shipping real digital products. *Paid.*
- [Uxcel](https://uxcel.com/) - Bite-size UX and UI courses with skill assessments. *Free + paid.*
- [Anthropic Academy: Learn](https://www.anthropic.com/learn) - Official courses on Claude, Claude Code, MCP and AI fluency. *Free.*
- [Elements of AI (University of Helsinki)](https://www.elementsofai.com/) - Free AI basics course with certificate. *Free.*
- [ChatGPT Prompt Engineering for Developers (DeepLearning.AI)](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/chatgpt-prompt-engineering-for-developers/) - Short prompting course with OpenAI. *Free.*
- [Google AI Essentials](https://grow.google/ai-essentials/) - Google's practical AI course with certificate. *Paid.*
- [GitHub Certifications](https://resources.github.com/learn/certifications/) - GitHub Foundations, Copilot and other certifications. *Paid exam.*
- [IBM SkillsBuild](https://skillsbuild.org/) - Free AI and tech courses with credentials. *Free.*
- [Figma for Edu: Prototyping with animations (workshop)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9lZ5GMXNyU) - Recorded monthly workshop led by Miggi. *Free.*
- [Figma for Edu: Working with typography systems (workshop)](https://youtu.be/rbxNV3_mb-A) - Recorded monthly workshop. *Free.*

## Books Online

- [Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/) - Matthew Butterick's free online typography book. *Free.*

## AI Engineering

- [Retrieval Augmented Generation (DeepLearning.AI)](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/retrieval-augmented-generation) - Zain Hasan's hands-on course: retrieval, vector databases, generation and evaluation of production RAG systems. First module free to preview. *Freemium.*
- [RAG From Scratch (LangChain)](https://github.com/langchain-ai/rag-from-scratch) - Notebooks with a video playlist that build RAG step by step: indexing, retrieval, generation. *Free, open source.*
- [Hugging Face LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course) - Free course on transformers and LLMs from Hugging Face. *Free.*
- [AI Engineering (Chip Huyen, O'Reilly)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/) - Building products on foundation models: evals, RAG, agents, fine-tuning. *Paid.*
- [Inference Engineering (Philip Kiely, Baseten)](https://www.baseten.co/inference-engineering/) - Book on serving models: GPUs, vLLM, quantisation, KV cache. *Free to read online.*

## Spanish and Portuguese

- [No Solo Usabilidad [Not Only Usability]](https://www.nosolousabilidad.com/) - Yusef Hassan Montero's long-running Spanish journal on usability, information architecture and interaction design. *Free.*
- [Torresburriel Estudio: Weblog](https://torresburriel.com/weblog/) - Daniel Torres Burriel's Spanish UX studio blog on research, UX strategy and teams; he leads the Spain chapter of UXPA. *Free.*
- [Interaction Latin America (IxDA)](https://ixda.org/initiatives/interaction-latin-america/) - IxDA's yearly conference for the Latin American design community, held since 2009 (first in São Paulo), talks in Spanish and Portuguese. *Free talks, paid tickets.*
- [Elisava](https://www.elisava.net/en) - Barcelona school of design and engineering: bachelor's and master's programmes, many taught in Spanish. *Paid.*
- [Aela School](https://aela.io/) - Brazilian online school for UX and product design, courses in Portuguese. *Paid.*
- [EBAC: Escola Britânica de Artes Criativas e Tecnologia [British School of Creative Arts and Technology]](https://ebaconline.com.br/) - Online school with UX/UI and product design courses in Portuguese. *Paid.*
- [UX / UI: Fundamentos para o design de interface [UX/UI: Interface design fundamentals]](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ux-ui-design-de-interface) - Introductory Coursera course in Portuguese by the Brazilian studio Taqtile: ideation, prototypes, wireframes, accessibility. *Free to audit.*
- [DIO: Formação UX Design [UX Design track]](https://www.dio.me/curso-ux-design) - Brazilian tech-education platform's UX design track with projects and a community, in Portuguese. *Free + paid.*
