## Type Foundries and Typography

- [Plau](https://plau.co/) - Rio type foundry of Rodrigo Saiani and Daniel Sabino; retail and custom typefaces. *Paid.*
- [Blackletra](https://blackletra.com/) - Brazilian foundry known for display and text families. *Paid.*
- [Outras Fontes (Other Fonts)](https://outrasfontes.com/) - Independent Brazilian type foundry. *Paid.*
- [Tipos Latinos (Latin Types)](https://www.tiposlatinos.com/) - Biennial exhibition of Latin American type design with online catalogues. *Free.*

## Design History and Archives

- [CASCA Archive](https://casca-archive.org/) - Graphic design and visual culture of Northeast Brazil from the 1950s: cordel, signage, posters. *Free.*
- [Enciclopédia Itaú Cultural (Itaú Cultural Encyclopedia)](https://enciclopedia.itaucultural.org.br/) - Biographies of Brazilian artists and designers: Aloísio Magalhães, Alexandre Wollner, Rogério Duarte, Ziraldo, Benício, Elifas Andreato. *Free.*
- [Hemeroteca Digital Brasileira (Brazilian Digital Newspaper Library)](https://bndigital.bn.gov.br/hemeroteca-digital/) - Digitised newspapers and magazines, including O Cruzeiro and Revista da Semana. *Free.*
- [Brasiliana Fotográfica (Brazilian Photography Portal)](https://brasilianafotografica.bn.gov.br/) - Historical photography from Brazilian collections. *Free.*
- [Biblioteca Brasiliana Guita e José Mindlin (Brasiliana Library, USP)](https://digital.bbm.usp.br/) - Rare Brazilian books, prints and periodicals online. *Free.*
- [ADG Brasil — Associação dos Designers Gráficos (Brazilian Graphic Designers Association)](https://www.adg.org.br/) - Runs the Bienal Brasileira de Design Gráfico and its catalogues. *Free.*
- [Ubu Editora](https://www.ubueditora.com.br/) - Publisher of design and art books, successor to Cosac Naify titles on Brazilian graphic design. *Paid.*
- [ESDI — Escola Superior de Desenho Industrial (Higher School of Industrial Design)](https://www.esdi.uerj.br/) - Rio school founded in 1963, the cradle of modern Brazilian design. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*

## Museums

- [MASP — Museu de Arte de São Paulo](https://masp.org.br/en) - Lina Bo Bardi's museum with glass easels. *Paid.*
- [Pinacoteca de São Paulo](https://pinacoteca.org.br/) - Brazilian art from the 19th century to today. *Paid.*
- [Museu da Casa Brasileira (Museum of the Brazilian House)](https://mcb.org.br/) - Design and architecture museum that runs the Prêmio Design MCB. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [MAM Rio — Museu de Arte Moderna do Rio de Janeiro](https://mam.rio/) - Modern art in Affonso Eduardo Reidy's building. *Paid.*
- [MAC USP — Museu de Arte Contemporânea](https://mac.usp.br/) - University of São Paulo contemporary art collection. *Free.*
- [Museu Afro Brasil Emanoel Araujo](https://www.museuafrobrasil.org.br/) - Afro-Brazilian art and history in Ibirapuera. *Paid.*
- [Instituto Tomie Ohtake](https://www.institutotomieohtake.org.br/) - Art, architecture and design exhibitions in São Paulo. *Free.*
- [Inhotim](https://www.inhotim.org.br/) - Contemporary art and botanical garden in Minas Gerais. *Paid.*
- [Museu Oscar Niemeyer](https://www.museuoscarniemeyer.org.br/) - Art museum in Curitiba designed by Niemeyer. *Paid.*
- [Museu do Amanhã (Museum of Tomorrow)](https://museudoamanha.org.br/) - Science museum by Santiago Calatrava in Rio. *Paid.*
- [Instituto Moreira Salles](https://ims.com.br/) - Photography, music and literature archives. *Free.*
- [Bienal de São Paulo](https://bienal.org.br/) - The second-oldest art biennial in the world, with its Wanda Svevo archive. *Free.*

## Architecture and Landscape

- [Instituto Bardi / Casa de Vidro (Glass House)](https://institutobardi.org/) - Lina Bo Bardi and Pietro Maria Bardi's house and archive. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Fundação Oscar Niemeyer](https://www.niemeyer.org.br/) - Niemeyer's works, drawings and archive. *Free.*
- [Sesc Pompeia](https://www.sescsp.org.br/unidades/pompeia/) - Lina Bo Bardi's converted factory turned cultural centre. *Free.*
- [Instituto Burle Marx](https://institutoburlemarx.org/) - Archive and legacy of landscape designer Roberto Burle Marx. *Free.*
- [Sítio Roberto Burle Marx](https://sitioburlemarx.org/) - Burle Marx's estate and plant collection in Rio. *Paid.*

## Cinema

- [Cinemateca Brasileira](https://cinemateca.org.br/) - Brazil's national film archive, including Cinema Novo posters. *Free.*
- [Mostra Internacional de Cinema em São Paulo](https://mostra.org/) - São Paulo International Film Festival, known for its commissioned posters. *Paid.*

## Advertising and Creative Industry

- [Clube de Criação](https://www.clubedecriacao.com.br/) - Brazilian creative club and annual for advertising and design. *Free.*
