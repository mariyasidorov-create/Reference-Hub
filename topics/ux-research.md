## Research and Guidelines

- [Baymard Institute](https://baymard.com/) - E-commerce usability research: forms, checkout. *Partly paid.*
- [Smart Interface Design Patterns](https://smart-interface-design-patterns.com/) - Vitaly Friedman's blog and courses on interface patterns. *Partly paid.*
- [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) - Psychological principles behind UX. *Free.*
- [Growth.Design](https://growth.design/) - Product teardowns told as comics. *Free.*
- [UX Matters](https://www.uxmatters.com/) - Articles on UX practice. *Free.*
- [Smashing Magazine](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/) - Articles on UX and front-end development. *Free.*
