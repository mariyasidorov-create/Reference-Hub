## Titles and Typography

- [Watch the Titles](https://www.watchthetitles.com/) - Archive of film and TV title sequences. *Free.*
- [Typeset in the Future](https://typesetinthefuture.com/) - Dave Addey's deep dives into typography in science-fiction films. *Free.*
- [The Movie Title Stills Collection](https://annyas.com/screenshots/) - Title-card screenshots of classic films. *Free.*

## Frames and Stills

- [Film-Grab](https://film-grab.com/) - High-quality stills from thousands of films. *Free.*
- [ShotDeck](https://shotdeck.com/) - Searchable library of film frames by colour, lighting and lens. *Paid.*

## Posters

- [Posteritati](https://www.posteritati.com/) - Gallery and archive of original international film posters. *Free.*
- [IMP Awards](http://www.impawards.com/) - Database of film posters by year. *Free.*

## Archives and Institutions

- [BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/) - British Film Institute: archive, library, Sight and Sound. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/) - Paris film museum with poster and design collections. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Academy Museum of Motion Pictures](https://www.academymuseum.org/) - Los Angeles museum of film by the Academy. *Paid.*
- [Margaret Herrick Library Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.oscars.org/) - Posters, production art and photographs from the Academy library. *Free.*
- [Hong Kong Film Archive](https://www.filmarchive.gov.hk/en/) - Collections and research on Hong Kong cinema. *Free.*
- [Cinemateca Portuguesa](https://www.cinemateca.pt/) - Portuguese film museum in Lisbon with screenings and library. *Paid.*
- [The Criterion Collection](https://www.criterion.com/) - Restored classic films with essays and designed covers. *Paid.*
- [MUBI](https://mubi.com/) - Curated streaming of art-house cinema, with Notebook magazine. *Paid.*
