## Museums and Archives

- [Web Design Museum](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/) - Curated screenshots and videos of websites, apps and software from 1991 to the mid-2000s, by Petr Kovář. *Free.*
- [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/) - Archived versions of websites, including early Apple and Microsoft homepages. *Free.*
- [The First Website (CERN)](https://info.cern.ch/) - Restoration of the first website from 1991. *Free.*
- [Version Museum](https://www.versionmuseum.com/) - Visual history of websites, operating systems and apps over time. *Free.*
- [GUIdebook](https://guidebookgallery.org/) - Gallery of historical graphical user interfaces. *Free.*
- [Toasty Tech GUI Gallery](http://toastytech.com/guis/) - Screenshots of historical operating systems and GUIs. *Free.*
- [Computer History Museum](https://www.computerhistory.org/) - Museum in Mountain View with online exhibitions on computing and the web. *Free online.*

## GeoCities and the Personal Web

- [Cameron's World](https://www.cameronsworld.net/) - Collage of GeoCities fragments from 1994–2009. *Free.*
- [One Terabyte of Kilobyte Age](https://oneterabyteofkilobyteage.tumblr.com/) - Screenshots of rescued GeoCities pages rendered in period browsers. *Free.*
- [GeoCities Research Institute](https://blog.geocities.institute/) - Research blog on the GeoCities archive. *Free.*
- [Space Jam (1996)](https://www.spacejam.com/1996/) - The original 1996 movie website, still online. *Free.*
- [Neocities](https://neocities.org/) - Free hosting continuing the personal homepage tradition. *Free.*

## Books

- [Rob Ford, Julius Wiedemann — Web Design: The Evolution of the Digital World 1990–Today](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21655422W) - 640-page Taschen history of web design through more than 200 websites. Book page on Open Library (editions, library loans); publisher: taschen.com. *Book.*
