## International

- [Red Dot Design Award](https://www.red-dot.org/) - Product, brand and communication design awards with an online winners archive. *Free.*
- [iF Design Award](https://ifdesign.com/) - Hanover award with a searchable database of winning projects since 1954. *Free.*
- [D&AD Awards](https://www.dandad.org/) - London awards for design and advertising; the Pencil archive is a reference library. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [ADC Annual Awards (The One Club)](https://www.oneclub.org/) - The Art Directors Club awards since 1921. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Type Directors Club](https://www.tdc.org/) - Typography and type design competitions with yearly winners. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Cannes Lions](https://www.canneslions.com/) - International festival of creativity in advertising. *Paid.*
- [The Webby Awards](https://www.webbyawards.com/) - Awards for websites, apps, video and AI, with winner galleries. *Free.*
- [Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/) - Yearly awards for the best-designed apps and games on Apple platforms. *Free.*
- [Core77 Design Awards](https://designawards.core77.com/) - Product, interaction and service design awards with full project write-ups. *Free.*
- [Pentawards](https://pentawards.com/) - Packaging design awards with a winners gallery. *Free.*
- [Information is Beautiful Awards](https://www.informationisbeautifulawards.com/) - Data visualisation awards with a showcase of every shortlist. *Free.*

## Europe

- [German Design Award](https://www.german-design-council.de/en/) - German Design Council awards for product and communication design. *Free.*
- [Dutch Design Awards](https://www.dutchdesignawards.nl/) - Yearly awards during Dutch Design Week in Eindhoven. *Free.*
- [European Design Awards](https://europeandesign.org/) - Communication design awards judged by European design magazines. *Free.*

## Asia and Oceania

- [Good Design Award (Japan)](https://www.g-mark.org/en) - The Japanese G Mark since 1957, with a huge winners database. *Free.*
- [Golden Pin Design Award](https://www.goldenpin.org.tw/en/) - Taiwan's award for design in Chinese-speaking regions. *Free.*
- [Good Design Australia](https://good-design.org/) - Australia's oldest design awards. *Free.*
