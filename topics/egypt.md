## Museums and Collections

- [Museo Egizio (Egyptian Museum, Turin)](https://museoegizio.it/en/) - The oldest museum devoted entirely to ancient Egypt, with English guides. *Paid.*
- [Museo Egizio Collections Online](https://collezioni.museoegizio.it/en-GB) - Search objects and papyri of the Turin collection. *Free.*
- [Rivista del Museo Egizio (Museo Egizio Journal)](https://rivista.museoegizio.it/) - Open-access research journal of the Turin museum, mostly in English. *Free.*
- [Petrie Museum of Egyptian and Sudanese Archaeology](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/culture/petrie-museum) - UCL collection of everyday objects, textiles and early writing. *Free.*

## Research and Primary Sources

- [Griffith Institute](https://www.griffith.ox.ac.uk/) - Oxford archive of Egyptology, including Howard Carter's Tutankhamun records. *Free.*
- [Digital Giza](http://giza.fas.harvard.edu/) - Harvard's archive of photos, plans and records of the Giza pyramids. *Free.*
- [Theban Mapping Project](https://thebanmappingproject.com/) - Maps, plans and photographs of the Valley of the Kings. *Free.*
- [ISAC Publications (University of Chicago)](https://isac.uchicago.edu/research/publications) - Hundreds of Egyptology books free as PDFs from the Oriental Institute. *Free.*

## Hieroglyphs and Writing

- [Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae](https://thesaurus-linguae-aegyptiae.de/) - Dictionary and text corpus of the Egyptian language. *Free.*
- [JSesh](https://jsesh.qenherkhopeshef.org/) - Free editor for typesetting hieroglyphic texts. *Free.*
- [Noto Sans Egyptian Hieroglyphs](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+Egyptian+Hieroglyphs) - Free Unicode font with the full hieroglyph block. *Free.*
