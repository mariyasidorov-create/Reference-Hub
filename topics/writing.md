## UX Writing

- [Mailchimp Content Style Guide](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/) - The classic product content style guide. *Free.*
- [Google Developer Documentation Style Guide](https://developers.google.com/style) - Clear technical writing rules. *Free.*
- [Readymag: How to Create an Awesome Long Read](https://blog.readymag.com/how-to-create-an-awesome-long-read-3b0a040f5416) - Layout and pacing of long-form web stories. *Free.*

## Books

- [Microcopy: The Complete Guide (Kinneret Yifrah)](https://www.microcopybook.com/) - The best book on interface microcopy, 2nd edition. *Book to buy or borrow.*
- [Writing Is Designing (Michael J. Metts, Andy Welfle)](https://rosenfeldmedia.com/books/writing-is-designing/) - Words as part of the user experience. *Book to buy or borrow.*
- [Strategic Writing for UX (Torrey Podmajersky)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/strategic-writing-for/9781492049388/) - Voice charts and UX text patterns. *Book to buy or borrow.*
- [Content Design (Sarah Winters)](https://contentdesign.london/) - The book and school that defined content design. *Book to buy or borrow.*

## Guidelines

- [Apple HIG: Writing](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing) - Apple's rules for interface text. *Free.*
- [Material 3: Content design](https://m3.material.io/foundations/content-design/overview) - Google's guidance on UX writing. *Free.*
- [Polaris: Voice and tone (Shopify)](https://polaris-react.shopify.com/content/voice-and-tone) - Shopify's content guidelines. *Free.*
- [GOV.UK: Content design](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design) - Writing for public services. *Free.*
- [figma-ux-writing-style (Figma skill)](https://www.figma.com/community/skill/67176/figma-ux-writing-style) - Figma's own UX writing style as an agent skill. *Free.*

## Learning

- [IxDF: UX Writing](https://ixdf.org/literature/topics/ux-writing) - Interaction Design Foundation articles on UX writing. *Free.*
- [UX Writing Hub](https://uxwritinghub.com/) - Courses and articles on UX writing. *Freemium.*
