## Books and Guides

- [Product-Led Growth (Wes Bush)](https://productled.com/book) - The PLG guide, free to read online, 2nd edition. *Free.*
- [Startup Metrics for Pirates (Dave McClure)](https://www.slideshare.net/dmc500hats/startup-metrics-for-pirates-long-version) - The AARRR funnel deck that started pirate metrics. *Free.*
- [UX Design KPI Examples (Eleken)](https://www.eleken.co/blog-posts/ux-design-kpi-examples-learn-how-to-measure-user-experience) - How to choose and measure UX KPIs. *Free.*
- [How Tinder Designs Product (ADPList)](https://adplist.substack.com/p/how-tinder-designs-product-and-why) - Growth design at Tinder. *Free.*
