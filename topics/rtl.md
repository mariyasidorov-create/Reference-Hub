## Arabic Typography and Type

- [Bahia Shehab, Haytham Nawar — A History of Arab Graphic Design (AUC Press, 2020)](https://aucpress.com/9789774168918) - First book-length history of Arab graphic design, with more than 600 illustrations. *Paid, table of contents online.*
- [Titus Nemeth — Arabic Type-Making in the Machine Age (Brill, 2017)](https://brill.com/search?q=Arabic+Type-Making+in+the+Machine+Age) - Arabic type from the Linotype to digital, 1908–1993, with a chapter on Persian type. *Paid.*
- [Huda Smitshuijzen AbiFarès — Arabic Typography: A Comprehensive Sourcebook](https://www.saqibooks.com/) - Standard sourcebook on Arabic typography. *Paid.*
- [Khatt Foundation](https://www.khtt.net/) - Huda AbiFarès' centre for Arabic typography: projects, articles, books. *Free.*
- [29LT (Pascal Zoghbi)](https://www.29lt.com/) - Arabic and multi-script typefaces, articles on Arabic type design. *Free to browse.*
- [Mamoun Sakkal](https://www.sakkal.com/) - Calligrapher and type designer; articles on Arabic script styles. *Free.*
- [Google Fonts — Arabic](https://fonts.google.com/?subset=arabic) - Free Arabic typefaces for web and interfaces. *Free.*

## Hebrew Typography and Type

- [AlefAlefAlef](https://alefalefalef.co.il/en/) - Hebrew type foundry and magazine, reviving historical typefaces. *Free to read.*
- [Hebrew Typography in German-Speaking Regions (TH Köln)](https://www.gm.th-koeln.de/hebrewtype/englisch/e_willkommen.html) - Research project on Hebrew typefaces cast in German-speaking regions, 16th century to 1939. *Free.*
- [Ada Yardeni — The Book of Hebrew Script](https://www.oakknoll.com/) - History, palaeography, script styles, calligraphy and design of Hebrew letters. *Paid.*
- [Ismar David — The Hebrew Letter: Calligraphic Variations](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Ismar+David+The+Hebrew+Letter) - Calligraphy by the designer of David Hebrew. *Paid.*
- [National Library of Israel — Ktiv (Hebrew Manuscripts)](https://nli.org.il/en/discover/manuscripts/hebrew-manuscripts/about) - Digitised Hebrew manuscripts from libraries around the world. *Free, research and personal use.*
- [Google Fonts — Hebrew](https://fonts.google.com/?subset=hebrew) - Free typefaces with Hebrew. *Free.*

## RTL Layout and Interfaces

- [Apple HIG — Right to left](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/right-to-left) - Mirroring interfaces, icons, numerals and text on Apple platforms. *Free.*
- [Material Design — Bidirectionality](https://m2.material.io/design/usability/bidirectionality.html) - What to mirror and what not to in RTL interfaces. *Free.*
- [W3C — Arabic & Persian Layout Requirements (alreq)](https://www.w3.org/TR/alreq/) - Requirements for typesetting Arabic and Persian text. *Free.*
- [W3C — Hebrew Layout Requirements (hlreq)](https://w3c.github.io/hlreq/) - Requirements for typesetting Hebrew text. *Free.*
- [Ahmad Shadeed — RTL Styling 101](https://rtlstyling.com/) - In-depth guide to RTL styling in CSS. *Free.*
- [MDN — CSS logical properties](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/CSS_logical_properties_and_values) - CSS logical properties, the basis of RTL layouts. *Free.*
- [RTLCSS](https://rtlcss.com/) - Tool that converts LTR CSS to RTL automatically. *Free.*

## Calligraphy and Art History

- [Met — Islamic Calligraphy (Annemarie Schimmel, 1992)](https://www.metmuseum.org/met-publications/islamic-calligraphy-the-metropolitan-museum-of-art-bulletin-v-50-no-1-summer-1992) - Metropolitan Museum bulletin on Islamic calligraphy. *Free PDF.*
- [Met — Masterpieces from the Department of Islamic Art](https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/Masterpieces_from_the_Department_of_Islamic_Art_in_The_Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art.pdf) - Catalogue of the Met's Islamic collection by region and period. *Free PDF.*
- [Met — Art of the Islamic World: Arabic Script and the Art of Calligraphy](https://www.metmuseum.org/learn/educators/curriculum-resources/art-of-the-islamic-world/unit-two) - Teaching unit on Arabic script styles. *Free PDF.*
- [Annemarie Schimmel — Calligraphy and Islamic Culture](https://nyupress.org/) - Classic study of calligraphy in Islamic culture, including Persian nastaliq. *Paid.*
- [Sheila Blair — Islamic Calligraphy](https://edinburghuniversitypress.com/) - Comprehensive history of Islamic calligraphy. *Paid.*
- [Robert Hillenbrand — Islamic Art and Architecture](https://thamesandhudson.com/) - Concise history of Islamic art (World of Art series). *Paid.*
- [Bahia Shehab — A Thousand Times No: The Visual History of Lam-Alif](https://www.kehrerverlag.com/) - A thousand variations of the lam-alif ligature through history. *Paid.*

- [Aga Khan Museum](https://agakhanmuseum.org/) - Islamic art and manuscripts, online collection. *Free.*
- [Chester Beatty](https://chesterbeatty.ie/) - Qur'anic and Persian manuscripts in Dublin. *Free.*
- [Israel Museum](https://www.imj.org.il/en) - Jewish art, manuscripts and Israeli design. *Free.*
- [Arab Image Foundation](https://arabimagefoundation.org/) - Photography archive of the Arab world. *Free.*
- [Palestine Poster Project Archives](https://www.palestineposterproject.org/) - Thousands of posters with Arabic typography. *Free.*
