## Portugal

- [Faculdade de Belas-Artes da Universidade de Lisboa (Faculty of Fine Arts, University of Lisbon)](https://www.belasartes.ulisboa.pt/) - Fine arts, communication design and equipment design in Chiado. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Faculdade de Belas Artes da Universidade do Porto (Faculty of Fine Arts, University of Porto)](https://www.fba.up.pt/) - Fine arts and communication design in Porto. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Faculdade de Arquitetura da Universidade de Lisboa (Faculty of Architecture, University of Lisbon)](https://www.fa.ulisboa.pt/) - Architecture, urbanism, fashion and product design. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [Ar.Co — Centro de Arte e Comunicação Visual (Ar.Co Centre for Art and Visual Communication)](https://arco.pt/) - Independent art school in Lisbon since 1973. *Paid entry.*
- [IADE — Faculdade de Design, Tecnologia e Comunicação (IADE Faculty of Design, Technology and Communication)](https://www.iade.europeia.pt/) - Design school of Universidade Europeia in Lisbon. *Paid entry.*
- [EDIT. Disruptive Digital Education](https://edit.com.pt/) - UX/UI, digital and creative courses in Lisbon and Porto. *Paid entry.*
- [ESMAD — Escola Superior de Media Artes e Design (School of Media Arts and Design)](https://www.esmad.ipp.pt/) - Polytechnic of Porto school of media arts and design. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [EAAD — Escola de Arquitetura, Arte e Design da Universidade do Minho (School of Architecture, Art and Design, University of Minho)](https://www.eaad.uminho.pt/) - Architecture, art and design in Guimarães. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [MODATEX](https://www.modatex.pt/) - Professional fashion and textile training. *Paid entry.*

## Europe

- [Royal College of Art](https://www.rca.ac.uk/) - Postgraduate art and design university in London. *Paid entry.*
- [Central Saint Martins (UAL)](https://www.arts.ac.uk/colleges/central-saint-martins) - Art, design, fashion and communication in King's Cross. *Paid entry.*
- [Chelsea College of Arts (UAL)](https://www.arts.ac.uk/colleges/chelsea-college-of-arts) - Fine art, interior and textile design next to Tate Britain. *Paid entry.*
- [Slade School of Fine Art (UCL)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/slade/) - Fine art school of University College London. *Paid entry.*
- [Royal School of Needlework](https://royal-needlework.org.uk/) - Hand embroidery school at Hampton Court Palace since 1872. *Paid entry.*
- [Design Academy Eindhoven](https://www.designacademy.nl/) - Conceptual product and social design, Netherlands. *Paid entry.*
- [Gerrit Rietveld Academie](https://rietveldacademie.nl/) - Art and design academy in Amsterdam. *Paid entry.*
- [KABK — Royal Academy of Art, The Hague](https://www.kabk.nl/en) - Graphic design and type design (Type and Media). *Paid entry.*
- [ECAL — École cantonale d'art de Lausanne (Lausanne University of Art and Design)](https://ecal.ch/en/) - Swiss school of graphic, product and photography design. *Paid entry.*

## Outside Europe

- [Rhode Island School of Design (RISD)](https://www.risd.edu/) - Art and design college in Providence, with the RISD Museum. *Paid entry.*
- [Parsons School of Design](https://www.newschool.edu/parsons/) - Design school of The New School, New York. *Paid entry.*
- [School of Visual Arts (SVA)](https://sva.edu/) - Art, design and illustration in New York. *Paid entry.*
- [Pratt Institute](https://www.pratt.edu/) - Art, design and architecture in Brooklyn. *Paid entry.*
- [CalArts — California Institute of the Arts](https://calarts.edu/) - Art, film, animation and graphic design; free courses on Coursera. *Paid entry.*
- [ArtCenter College of Design](https://www.artcenter.edu/) - Industrial, transportation and graphic design in Pasadena. *Paid entry.*
- [Musashino Art University (武蔵野美術大学)](https://www.musabi.ac.jp/en/) - Tokyo art and design university with a design museum and library. *Paid entry.*
