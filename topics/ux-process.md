## Core Books

- [About Face: The Essentials of Interaction Design](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/About+Face%3A+The+Essentials+of+Interaction+Design%2C+4th+Edition-p-9781118766576) - Alan Cooper's goal-directed design; its chapters are the skeleton of the tag tree below. *Book to buy or borrow.*
- [Designing for Behavior Change](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-for-behavior/9781492056027/) - Stephen Wendel on applying behavioural science to products, 2nd edition. *Book to buy or borrow.*
- [Articulating Design Decisions](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/articulating-design-decisions/9781098117863/) - Tom Greever on presenting and defending design to stakeholders, 2nd edition. *Book to buy or borrow.*
- [Hooked](https://www.nirandfar.com/hooked/) - Nir Eyal's Hook Model for habit-forming products. *Book to buy or borrow.*

## Goals and Mental Models

- [NN/g: Mental Models](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mental-models/) - What mental models are and why designs should match them. *Free.*

## Product Behaviour

- [Fogg Behavior Model](https://behaviormodel.org/) - B = MAP: motivation, ability and prompt, by BJ Fogg. *Free.*

## Digital Etiquette

- [Calm Technology](https://calmtech.com/) - Amber Case's principles for technology that respects attention. *Free.*

## Platform and Posture

- [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/) - Design guidance for iOS, macOS, watchOS, visionOS and tvOS. *Free.*
- [Material Design 3](https://m3.material.io/) - Google's design system for Android, web and wearables. *Free.*
- [Wear OS design guidelines](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/wear) - Designing glanceable apps, tiles and complications for watches. *Free.*
- [Alexa Design Guide](https://developer.amazon.com/en-US/docs/alexa/alexa-design/get-started.html) - How to design voice conversations and multimodal skills. *Free.*
