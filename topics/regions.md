## The Americas

- [Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/) - US national design museum in New York. *Paid.*
- [National Museum of the American Indian](https://americanindian.si.edu/) - Smithsonian museum of Native cultures of the Americas. *Free.*
- [Museo Nacional de Antropología (National Museum of Anthropology, Mexico)](https://www.mna.inah.gob.mx/) - Aztec, Maya and other pre-Columbian collections. *Paid.*
- [Museo Larco](https://www.museolarco.org/en/) - Pre-Columbian art of Peru with an online catalogue. *Paid.*
- [Dumbarton Oaks: Pre-Columbian Studies](https://www.doaks.org/research/pre-columbian) - Harvard research centre with free publications on pre-Columbian art. *Free.*
- [ICAA Documents of Latin American and Latino Art](https://icaa.mfah.org/) - Museum of Fine Arts, Houston archive of primary sources in English and Spanish. *Free.*
- [MALBA](https://www.malba.org.ar/en/) - Latin American art museum in Buenos Aires. *Paid.*
- [MASP — Museu de Arte de São Paulo](https://masp.org.br/en) - Lina Bo Bardi's museum with glass easels. *Paid.*
- [National Gallery of Canada](https://www.gallery.ca/) - Canadian and Indigenous art in Ottawa. *Paid.*

## Africa

- [Zeitz MOCAA](https://zeitzmocaa.museum/) - Contemporary art from Africa in a converted grain silo, Cape Town. *Paid.*
- [National Museum of African Art (Smithsonian)](https://africa.si.edu/) - Traditional and modern African art with online collection. *Free.*
- [Contemporary And (C&)](https://contemporaryand.com/) - Magazine on contemporary art from Africa and the diaspora. *Free.*

## Oceania

- [National Gallery of Australia](https://nga.gov.au/) - Australian and Indigenous art in Canberra. *Free.*
- [Powerhouse Collection](https://collection.powerhouse.com.au/) - Sydney museum of applied arts and design, fully online. *Free.*
- [Te Papa Collections Online](https://collections.tepapa.govt.nz/) - New Zealand's national collection, including Māori taonga. *Free.*

## East Asia

- [The Palace Museum (故宫博物院)](https://en.dpm.org.cn/) - Forbidden City collection with a digital catalogue. *Paid.*
- [National Palace Museum (國立故宮博物院)](https://www.npm.gov.tw/en/) - Taipei collection of Chinese imperial art, with open data images. *Paid.*
- [Chinese Text Project](https://ctext.org/) - Pre-modern Chinese texts from the oracle bones onwards, with translations. *Free.*
- [International Dunhuang Programme](https://idp.bl.uk/) - Manuscripts and paintings from the Silk Road. *Free.*
- [National Museum of Korea](https://www.museum.go.kr/site/eng/home) - Korean art and archaeology in Seoul. *Free.*
- [MMCA — National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea](https://www.mmca.go.kr/eng/) - Korean modern art and design. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
- [ColBase (Japan National Museums)](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/?locale=en) - Collections of Japan's four national museums. *Free.*
- [Japan Search](https://jpsearch.go.jp/?lang=en) - Portal to Japanese digital archives. *Free.*
- [21_21 DESIGN SIGHT](https://www.2121designsight.jp/en/) - Tokyo design museum founded by Issey Miyake. *Paid.*
- [ginza graphic gallery (ggg)](https://www.dnpfcp.jp/gallery/ggg/) - Tokyo gallery of graphic design run by DNP. *Free.*
- [M+](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/) - Hong Kong museum of visual culture, design and moving image. *Paid.*

## Southeast Asia

- [National Gallery Singapore](https://www.nationalgallery.sg/) - Southeast Asian modern art. *Paid.*
- [Roots.sg](https://www.roots.gov.sg/) - Singapore heritage collections online. *Free.*
- [Islamic Arts Museum Malaysia](https://www.iamm.org.my/) - Islamic arts from across Asia in Kuala Lumpur. *Paid.*
- [ILHAM Gallery](https://www.ilhamgallery.com/) - Malaysian modern and contemporary art in Kuala Lumpur. *Free.*
