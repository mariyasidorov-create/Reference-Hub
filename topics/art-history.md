## Timelines and Overviews

- [The Story of Art (Phaidon)](https://www.phaidon.com/store/art/the-story-of-art-9780714832470/) - E. H. Gombrich's classic history of art; the chapter list is the skeleton of this topic. *Book to buy or borrow.*
- [Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History (The Met)](https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/) - Essays and timelines by region and period, written by Met curators. *Free.*
- [Smarthistory](https://smarthistory.org/) - Free art history textbook with videos, ordered by period like Gombrich. *Free.*
- [Khan Academy: Arts and Humanities](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/art-history) - Smarthistory courses from prehistory to today. *Free.*

## Image Databases

- [Web Gallery of Art](https://www.wga.hu/) - European painting and sculpture from 1000 to 1900 with commentary. *Free.*
- [Google Arts & Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/) - High-resolution artworks and virtual tours from thousands of museums. *Free.*
- [Europeana](https://www.europeana.eu/en) - Millions of digitised works from European museums, libraries and archives. *Free.*
