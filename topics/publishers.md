## Art and Design Publishers

- [Rizzoli](https://www.rizzoliusa.com/) - New York publisher of art, fashion, design and interiors books. *Paid.*
- [Unit Editions](https://www.uniteditions.com/) - Graphic design monographs and archives in small runs. *Paid.*
- [Gestalten](https://gestalten.com/) - Berlin publisher of visual culture and design. *Paid.*
- [Princeton Architectural Press](https://www.papress.com/) - Architecture and graphic design books. *Paid.*
- [Niggli](https://niggli.ch/) - Swiss publisher of typography and the Swiss style. *Paid.*
- [Counter-Print](https://counter-print.co.uk/) - Small publisher and shop of graphic design books. *Paid.*
- [Spector Books](https://spectorbooks.com/) - Leipzig publisher of art, design and theory. *Paid.*
- [Ad Marginem (Ад Маргинем)](https://admarginem.ru/) - Moscow publisher of art and theory, partner of Garage. *Paid.*
- [Phaidon](https://www.phaidon.com/) - Art, design, architecture and photography books, including The Story of Art. *Paid.*
- [Taschen](https://www.taschen.com/) - Illustrated books from affordable basics to collector's editions. *Paid.*
- [Thames & Hudson](https://thamesandhudson.com/) - London art publisher with the World of Art series. *Paid.*
- [Lars Müller Publishers](https://www.lars-mueller-publishers.com/) - Swiss publisher of design, typography and architecture. *Paid.*
- [Hyphen Press](https://hyphenpress.co.uk/) - London publisher of typography and design history. *Paid.*
- [The MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/) - Design, media and architecture titles, many open access. *Mixed: free site, paid entry.*
