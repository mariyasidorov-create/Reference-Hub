## Inspiration

- [Really Good Emails](https://reallygoodemails.com/) - Thousands of email designs by type and brand. *Free.*
- [Milled](https://milled.com/) - Searchable archive of brand newsletters, strong on e-commerce. *Free.*
- [Email Love](https://emaillove.com/) - Curated email designs with Figma templates. *Freemium.*

## Tools

- [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/) - Newsletter platform for writing and growing lists. *Freemium.*

## Code and Testing

- [Can I email](https://www.caniemail.com/) - Which HTML and CSS features work in which email clients. *Free.*
- [Good Email Code](https://www.goodemailcode.com/) - Accessible email HTML patterns by Mark Robbins. *Free.*
- [MJML](https://mjml.io/) - Framework for responsive email. *Free.*
- [React Email](https://react.email/) - Build emails with React components. *Free.*
- [Accessible Email](https://www.accessible-email.org/) - Check emails for accessibility issues. *Free.*

- [Litmus Blog](https://www.litmus.com/blog) - Email design and marketing research. *Free.*
